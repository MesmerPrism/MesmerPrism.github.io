Using the stimulus

Open this page in Quest Browser over HTTPS, then select Enter VR and use your hands. Desktop preview uses an animated rig. Missing live hands are hidden until tracking returns.

DHS moves the hand particles; SHD moves the sphere particles. Static hands still follow your pose. Coherence and energy select the four original stimulus conditions. Pause freezes oscillator motion while tracking continues.

There is no experiment recording or sensor connection. In the desktop view, drag the canvas to look around. Use Recenter after moving to a new position.
