Rusty Launcher Lite Privacy Policy

Rusty Launcher Lite is developed and published by Viscereality. This policy explains how Rusty Launcher Lite processes information locally on a Meta Quest headset.

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Scope

This privacy policy applies only to Rusty Launcher Lite, a Meta Quest application developed and published by Viscereality.

Information used on the headset

Rusty Launcher Lite processes only the information needed to display its launcher and apply user-selected preferences:

  • labels, package and activity names, and icons for enabled apps that publish a launchable front door;
  • whether ordinary Wi-Fi is currently on or off; and
  • favorites, bounded tags, search text, and per-app Wi-Fi preferences selected by the user.

Rusty Launcher Lite does not create an account or identity. It does not read Wi-Fi network names, passwords, addresses, traffic, or Wi-Fi debugging state, and it never changes Wi-Fi.

Collection and sharing

Rusty Launcher Lite contains no ads, analytics, telemetry, account service, server API, or Internet permission. Information is processed locally on the headset and is not transmitted to Viscereality or any third party. Viscereality does not collect, sell, rent, share, or use this information for advertising or profiling.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Favorites, tags, search text, and Wi-Fi preferences are stored in Rusty Launcher Lite's private app data on the headset. They remain until the user clears the app's storage or uninstalls the app. Either action deletes these local preferences. Android owns installed-app information and Wi-Fi state; Rusty Launcher Lite reads their current values when needed rather than uploading or retaining a separate copy.

Children and purchases

Rusty Launcher Lite has no purchases, subscriptions, social features, user-generated content, advertising, or account system. Viscereality does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults through Rusty Launcher Lite.

Changes and contact

Viscereality maintains this policy for Rusty Launcher Lite. Material changes will be published at this same URL with a revised last-updated date. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to mesmerprism@gmail.com.
